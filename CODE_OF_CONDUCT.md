# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can report a bug, propose a
change, or ask a naive question without being made to feel small for it. That
applies to everyone taking part, regardless of experience, background, identity
or how they make their living.

## What this looks like in practice

- Review the code, not the person. "This misses the retry case" is useful;
  "did you even read the docs" is not.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Most bad
  patches are a misunderstanding of context, not carelessness.
- A naive question is a documentation bug. Answer it, then fix the docs.
- Disagree with reasons. This project has a written charter — `CLAUDE.md` — so
  most design arguments can point at something concrete rather than at taste.
- Accept a decision once it is made, or open an issue to revisit it. Re-fighting
  a settled question in every pull request wears people out.

Unacceptable, and the reason this document exists: harassment of any kind,
personal or demeaning remarks, publishing someone's private information, and
sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to **contact@webaround.ro**. Reports are handled
confidentially. If the report concerns a maintainer, say so — it will be handled
by someone else.

Maintainers may warn, remove a comment, or block an account. We will say what
happened and why, to the people involved.
